# Kata

![Kata logo](../assets/kata.svg)

Initial idea and scope, 2 October 2026.

A meta-skill, or skill compendium, on **how to make good slides**. A collection of patterns that seem to work for presenting particular forms or types of data.

To be used together with Lys, as **kata-lys**, or with the Futurice brand skill.

## Working direction

The practical analogy is PowerPoint SmartArt: recognise the form of the information, then choose a useful way to present it. Kata should offer concrete examples and enough judgment to choose and adapt a pattern.

**Gestalt psychology** is an inspiration for finding and explaining visual patterns that seem to work. Use it as a source of ideas for presenting information, rather than a prescriptive guideline.

Proposed division to explore: Kata supplies presentation patterns, Lys supplies slide behavior, and the brand skill supplies the visual identity. The packaging and boundaries are still open.

## Possible shape of a pattern

Starting proposal, to test with real examples:

- The form or type of data being presented, and what the audience needs to see in it.
- When the pattern is useful, and when it gets in the way.
- How to structure the message and arrange it on the slide.
- Why it might work, distinguishing supported psychology from design judgment or current taste.
- A concrete example, ideally with a before and after.

## Starting scope

Start with **3–5 basic examples of how to present data of a particular form or type**. The unit is a slide pattern. Include sequences only where the pattern itself unfolds over time; general deck storytelling is outside this initial scope.

Candidate examples, proposed rather than selected:

- **Comparison:** several options described by the same attributes.
- **Composition:** parts that make up a whole.
- **Hierarchy:** nested groups or levels.
- **Relationship:** entities connected through dependencies or flows.
- **Change over time:** values or states across time, potentially shown as a sequence when the unfolding is useful.

First step: choose 3–5 forms and develop a concrete worked slide example for each. Keep the examples usable with Lys or the Futurice brand skill.
