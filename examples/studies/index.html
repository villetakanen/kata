Reading around the work

Three approaches to typography and reading rhythm: quiet editorial, working notebook and exhibition catalogue. Round 2 compares Archivo, Newsreader and Literata. What colour is for

Adopted: paper reserved for example slides, a light reading surface, #111217 ink and Reasonable Colors raspberry accents. Round 4 compares original paper and three 50/50 mid-tone blends of original, warmer ivory and mineral against ceramic. Examples use toned-black reading ink, petrol headings and emphasis, and paper lettering on petrol text backgrounds. Rejected variants remain as history.
