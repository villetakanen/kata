Site colours

Use white for the page background, ceramic for reading surfaces and silicate black for text. Use raspberry for links and the wordmark underline.

An underlined link uses Raspberry 4, with Raspberry 5 on hover and a visible focus outline for keyboard use. Raspberry 3 is the decorative wordmark underline, not small text.

Example styling

Use paper backgrounds and petrol accents only inside examples.

Choose original paper (the default), warmer paper or mineral paper. Use one throughout a deck. The options are visual variations, not categories. Paper family decision.

Use silicate black for body text, captions and metadata. Use petrol for headings, underlines and emphasis. For text highlights, use paper-coloured lettering on petrol. Apply the background to the selected words, not the whole text block.

Open the three-paper Lys deck · Example palette decision

Petrol is lch(34 25 220). Its contrast on the paper family is approximately 7.07:1, 6.83:1 and 6.68:1; reversed lettering has the same ratio. Silicate black reading text exceeds 15:1. These are colour-pair checks, not a full accessibility certification.
