# Campus shuttle options

This is a fictional scenario; all figures are invented for the evaluation.

## Audience question

Which shuttle option meets the minimum range at the lowest upfront cost?

## Source facts

The procurement team requires a minimum range of **200 kilometres**. Costs
are fictional planning estimates in thousands of US dollars.

| Option | Range (km) | Upfront cost (USD thousands) | Estimated annual energy cost (USD thousands) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Alder | 210 | 38 | 4.2 |
| Birch | 240 | 44 | 3.9 |
| Cedar | 190 | 35 | 4.5 |
| Dogwood | 220 | 47 | 3.8 |

The annual energy estimates assume 18,000 kilometres of service per vehicle.
They do not include maintenance or charging-infrastructure costs. All figures
are estimates supplied by the same procurement worksheet; no uncertainty
ranges were provided.

## Constraints

- Make one slide for the procurement team.
- Compare every option using the same criteria and units.
- Identify the lowest upfront-cost option among those meeting the 200 km
  minimum; do not imply it is best on every criterion.
- Preserve the annual energy-cost assumption and excluded cost categories.
