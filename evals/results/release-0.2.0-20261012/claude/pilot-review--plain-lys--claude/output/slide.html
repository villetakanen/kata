The evidence supports

  • Deciding whether to run another measurement cycle, which the group can authorize.
  • Reporting the +6% median yield difference as a pilot result, shown with its spread (−3% to +14%).

It does not support

  • Saying lighting caused the yield differences: plots were not randomly assigned.
  • Wider installation on yield alone: installation cost and energy use were not measured.
  • Generalizing beyond these 18 plots, 3 sites and one winter cycle; crop mix and baseline conditions differed between sites.

Open for decision: another measurement cycle. Not supported yet: wider installation.
