# Home retrofit feedback summary

This is a fictional scenario; all figures are invented for the evaluation.

## Audience question

Which retrofit barrier is most common overall, and did service turnaround
improve?

## Source facts

Two partners sent separate summaries for a recent intake period. They did not
use a shared reporting form.

**Partner North**

- 40 homeowners completed a survey.
- Respondents could select more than one barrier.
- 28 selected “installer scheduling”; 17 selected “upfront cost”; 9 selected
  “unclear eligibility”. These counts overlap and sum to more than 40.
- No service-turnaround times were included.

**Partner South**

- 15 open support cases were reviewed; the total number of homeowners served
  in the period was not reported.
- 9 cases mentioned “waiting for an installer”. The review did not record
  whether other barriers were present.
- Turnaround time was available for 11 cases: median **8 days**. Four cases
  had no time recorded.
- A free-text note says “around two weeks last spring”; it does not identify
  the cases, sample size, or method used for that estimate.

There is no shared period definition, common denominator, or comparable
baseline measure across the partners.

## Constraints

- Make one slide for the program leads.
- Do not pool the two partners' counts or rank barriers overall without
  compatible denominators and a rule for overlapping selections.
- Do not claim turnaround improved: the recent partial median and the
  unmethoded “around two weeks last spring” note are not comparable measures.
- State which part of the question cannot be answered and what information is
  needed to answer it.
