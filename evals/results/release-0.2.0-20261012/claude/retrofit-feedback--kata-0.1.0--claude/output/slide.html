40 homeowners completed a survey. Respondents could select more than one barrier, so the bars overlap and do not sum to 100%.

Partner South: support cases

15 open cases reviewed. Homeowners served in the period was not reported, and other barriers were not recorded, so there is no ranking or population share.

  • Waiting for an installer 9 of 15 cases (60%)

Turnaround

  • North: no turnaround times were included.
  • South, “around two weeks last spring”: not plotted. The cases, sample size, and method are not given.

Most common barrier: within North, installer scheduling (28 of 40). There is no overall ranking, because the sources use different denominators and South does not record other barriers. Whether South’s “waiting for an installer” is the same category as North’s “installer scheduling” is not established.

Did turnaround improve? Cannot be determined. There is no comparable baseline: one recent partial median (8 days, 11 cases) and one unmethoded recollection.

Needed: shared barrier definitions and a rule for overlapping selections; a common denominator per partner for the same period; a shared period definition; and turnaround measured the same way (start and end points, n, missing handling) for a baseline and the current period from both partners.
