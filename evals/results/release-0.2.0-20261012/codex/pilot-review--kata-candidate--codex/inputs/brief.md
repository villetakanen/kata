# Riverbank greenhouse lighting pilot

This is a fictional scenario; all figures are invented for the evaluation.

## Audience question

What does the pilot evidence support the steering group doing next?

## Source facts

- A cooperative tested supplemental lighting on 18 greenhouse plots at three
  sites over one winter growing cycle.
- The reported median yield difference versus each site's reference plots was
  **+6%**. Individual plot differences ranged from **−3% to +14%**.
- Twelve of the 18 plots had a positive yield difference; six did not.
- The plots were not randomly assigned. Crop mix and baseline conditions
  differed between sites.
- The pilot did not measure installation cost or energy use.
- The steering group can authorize another measurement cycle, but has not
  approved wider installation.

## Constraints

- Make one slide for the steering group.
- Preserve the pilot size, duration, result, and limitations.
- Do not claim that lighting caused the observed yield differences.
- Do not recommend wider installation based on yield alone; cost and energy
  use have not been measured.
- The group can decide whether to run another measurement cycle.
