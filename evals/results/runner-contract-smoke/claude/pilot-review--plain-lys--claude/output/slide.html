Riverbank greenhouse lighting pilot · steering group

The pilot supports one more measurement cycle, not wider installation

What the pilot measured

  • 18 greenhouse plots at three sites, one winter growing cycle.
  • Median yield difference vs. each site's reference plots: +6%.
  • Individual plot differences: −3% to +14%.
  • 12 of 18 plots had a positive yield difference; 6 of 18 did not.

Supported next step

  • Authorize another measurement cycle. The group can make this decision now.

Not supported yet

  • Wider installation on yield alone. Installation cost and energy use were not measured, and no wider installation is approved.
  • A causal claim. Plots were not randomly assigned; crop mix and baseline conditions differed between sites. The pilot does not show that lighting caused these differences.

Scope: one cooperative pilot, 18 plots, 3 sites, 1 winter growing cycle. Fictional scenario; all figures are invented for the evaluation.
