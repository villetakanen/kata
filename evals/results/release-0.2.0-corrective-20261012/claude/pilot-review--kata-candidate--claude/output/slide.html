The pilot supports deciding whether to run another measurement cycle, not wider installation

What the pilot measured

  • A cooperative tested supplemental lighting on 18 greenhouse plots at three sites over one winter growing cycle.
  • Each plot's yield difference is measured against its own site's reference plots.
  • Plots were not randomly assigned. Crop mix and baseline conditions differed between sites.

The evidence supports

  • Deciding whether to authorize another measurement cycle. The steering group can make that decision.
  • Reporting the median yield difference (+6%) as a pilot result, with its −3% to +14% range.

The evidence does not support

  • Wider installation on yield alone. Installation cost and energy use were not measured.
  • Saying lighting caused the yield differences. Plots were not randomly assigned, and sites differed.
  • Expecting a gain on every plot. 6 of 18 plots did not have a positive difference.

Open decision for the steering group: whether to authorize another measurement cycle. Wider installation has not been approved.
