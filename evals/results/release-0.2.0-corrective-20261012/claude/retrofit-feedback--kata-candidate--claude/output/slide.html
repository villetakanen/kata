Did service turnaround improve?

Cannot tell. There is no comparable baseline. The only turnaround median (Partner South, 8 days) is a recent, partial figure, and the “around two weeks last spring” note gives no cases, sample size, or method. No improvement is claimed.

  • Partner South, recent: median 8 days, from 11 of 15 cases with a recorded time. 4 cases had no time recorded.
  • Partner South, free-text note: “around two weeks last spring”. The cases, sample size, and method are not identified, so this is not comparable with the median.
  • Partner North: no service-turnaround times were included.

Needed to answer the question

  • Overall barrier ranking: one barrier question for both partners; a rule for overlapping selections; a common denominator per source (homeowners served, respondents, or cases); full barrier coding for South’s cases; and a documented mapping between the partners’ categories. “Installer scheduling” and “waiting for an installer” are not assumed to be the same thing.
